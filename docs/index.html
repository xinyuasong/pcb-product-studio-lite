How it works

  1. Export your board from KiCad

    One command gives a GLB with copper, pads, mask and silkscreen as separate layers.

    kicad-cli pcb export glb -f -o board.glb --subst-models --include-tracks --include-pads --include-zones --include-silkscreen --include-soldermask board.kicad_pcb

  2. Import it and click Quick product shot

    The add-on lays the board flat, gives every layer a PCB material, builds a studio and frames a camera.

  3. Press F12

    A 1080p draft took 10 to 30 seconds in our test on an RTX 5060 laptop. Your time will vary with your GPU and board.
